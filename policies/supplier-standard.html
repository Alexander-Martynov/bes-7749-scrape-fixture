Supplier Code of Conduct

MARKER-SUPPLIER-REVISION-A-7749: This is revision A of the supplier standard.

Every supplier to Meridian Copper Holdings must uphold the standards set out below as a condition of doing business with us. These requirements extend to sub-contractors and to labour providers engaged on our sites.

Suppliers shall not use forced, bonded or child labour, shall pay at least the applicable legal minimum wage, and shall not charge recruitment fees to workers under any circumstance.

Suppliers shall maintain a documented health and safety management system and report any fatality or high-potential incident to us within twenty-four hours.

We audit high-risk suppliers at least once every two years and reserve the right to terminate any agreement where a breach is not remedied within an agreed corrective-action period.
