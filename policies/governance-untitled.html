Corporate Governance Framework

MARKER-NOTITLE-7749: This page deliberately carries no title element.

The Board of Meridian Copper Holdings retains overall stewardship of the company's conduct of business. It comprises nine directors, seven of whom are independent, and it meets no fewer than six times a year. The Audit Committee is composed entirely of independent directors and appoints the external auditor.

The Sustainability Committee reviews climate targets, tailings governance and the results of the annual human-rights risk assessment. It reports to the Board after every meeting.

Directors are required to disclose conflicts of interest before any deliberation and to recuse themselves from the relevant decision. Related-party transactions above a de minimis threshold require prior approval by the independent directors acting alone.
